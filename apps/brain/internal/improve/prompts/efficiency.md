This is an efficiency pass. The agent's runs go well; some cost far more, or take far longer, than others. Your aim is the smallest change that brings the costly runs down while every run stays as correct and as complete. The material has no failure patterns: "Why this pass" lists the reasons in their place, each a signature to cite, and "What each run cost" gives each run you may read with its tokens, steps, work time, tool time and waste marks.

- Correctness first. Propose nothing that could make a run less correct or less complete to make it cheaper. When in doubt, propose nothing, and say why in the report.
- Contrast: read the costliest runs beside the cheap ones on requests like them, and say in "why" what the costly runs did that the cheap ones did not: calling a lookup twice, retrying a call that could not succeed, reading a whole skill when one reference was enough.
- The saving: give "saves" as cost, time or both, and state the hypothesis as the saving you expect and why, for example "repeated calls to crm__lookup stop; tokens a run fall by about a fifth".
- A waste mark is a signal, not a verdict. A repeated call may be a poll, or a retry after a timeout, and be right.
- Only changes that make a run do less: instructions (a passage that removes a step, or says when not to call a tool), tool_note (when a tool's result is enough the first time), tools_narrow, skill_drop, limits (lower only), delegation (narrow only), pattern (only to react, the plainest loop), and skill_edit without a script. Never widen, raise or add anything, and never touch the model: propose_change refuses those.
